Overview

We integrate sensing, estimation and control on a simulated TurtleBot3 Waffle Pi in MuJoCo. The robot starts in one corner of a 4 m × 4 m room with four obstacles, navigates through waypoints using its 2D LiDAR, and docks at a charging station marked with an ArUco marker. The controller uses only the robot's estimated state, never the simulator's ground truth.

Changes to the supplied robot model:

  • The chassis collision mesh touched the floor and dragged with full friction, so the robot reached only about 50% of the commanded speed and could not turn in place. The chassis contact is now frictionless, so it behaves like the real ball casters.
  • The wheel motor gain was raised from 0.1 to 1. With 0.1 the wheels reached only about 75% of the commanded speed because of joint friction. With 1 they reach about 97%.
  • The model had no camera, LiDAR or encoders, so these were added at the positions of the real Raspberry Pi camera and LDS-01.

Model

State $x = [p_x, p_y, \theta]^T$ of {B} in {W}, input $u = [v, \omega]^T$. Differential-drive (unicycle) kinematics with wheel radius r = 0.033 m and wheel separation b = 0.288 m:

$$\dot p_x = v\cos\theta,\quad \dot p_y = v\sin\theta,\quad \dot\theta = \omega,\qquad v = \tfrac{r(\omega_R+\omega_L)}{2},\quad \omega = \tfrac{r(\omega_R-\omega_L)}{b}$$

Assumptions: no wheel slip, flat floor, motor dynamics fast compared with the controller. Checked in simulation: commanding 0.15 m/s gives 0.146 m/s, and commanding 0.5 rad/s gives 0.474 rad/s, with wheel speeds matching the kinematics exactly.

Discrete-time model for the estimator (Euler, Δt):

$$x_{k+1} = x_k + \Delta t \begin{bmatrix} v_k\cos\theta_k \\ v_k\sin\theta_k \\ \omega_k \end{bmatrix},\qquad F_k = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & -v_k\Delta t\sin\theta_k \\ 0 & 1 & v_k\Delta t\cos\theta_k \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

Tracking-error model for the controller, linearised about a reference $(v_r, \omega_r)$, with the error in the robot frame:

$$\dot e = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & \omega_r & 0 \\ -\omega_r & 0 & v_r \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} e + \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \delta u$$
